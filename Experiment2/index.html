About the Department

The Department of Computer Science Engineering at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur is committed to producing industry-ready engineers equipped with strong programming skills, analytical thinking, and problem-solving abilities. The department offers a curriculum that blends core computer science fundamentals with emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Web Development, and Cloud Computing — preparing students to excel in the ever-evolving tech industry.
